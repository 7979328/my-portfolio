About Me

I See the Story Behind Your Data

"Data without context is just noise. My job is to turn that noise into a narrative that drives real decisions."

I'm Muhammad Ubaid Khan — a certified BI Consultant, Power BI expert, and content writer based in Pakistan. For over 5 years I have been helping businesses, professionals, and organizations make sense of their data and communicate their expertise to the world.

My work sits at the intersection of two powerful disciplines: business intelligence and storytelling. I build dashboards that executives actually use — and I write content that audiences actually read.

Beyond the technical work, I am passionate about helping people grow — in their careers, their confidence, and their impact. That is why I write about motivation, consistency, and strategy alongside data and analytics.
